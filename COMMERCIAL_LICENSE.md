# Commercial & Government Production Licensing — $DDD Games

**Source is public. Production pays.**

| Track | Rights | Cost |
| ----- | ------ | ---- |
| **Section A** | Read, audit, personal non-commercial play, research | Free |
| **Section B** | Production, SaaS, commercial redistribution, government ops, paid tournaments, commercial game economy | **Paid written license** |

Full legal text: [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)

---

## Why this model

| Goal | How |
| ---- | --- |
| Public validation & trust | Source visible; playable arena can be inspected |
| Stop free SaaS / white-label clones | Production needs Section B |
| Government must pay to operationalize | Explicit Section A(d) / Section B |
| Contributors welcome | CLA assigns rights so dual-license stays enforceable |
| Max value for founder | Copyright + dual license + trademarks reserved |

This is **source-available dual licensing**, not OSI open source.  
OSI open source cannot require payment for ordinary production use. That is intentional and is how you keep upside.

---

## Who needs Section B

- Studios or companies hosting $DDD (or a fork) for real users
- Paid tournaments, seasons, or leaderboard products
- App stores / paid clients embedding substantial first-party code
- Commercial token / NFT / game-economy products built on this codebase
- Government / public agencies in operational deployment
- White-label or rebranded forks offered as a product

## Who does not (Section A only)

- You playing the arena locally for fun
- Auditors and security researchers
- Students / academic non-commercial research
- Contributors opening PRs under the CLA

| Tier | Direction |
| ---- | --------- |
